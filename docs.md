I. Company Background
Maxxbrands International INC Interior Source is a business engaged in the sale of furniture products sourced from international suppliers. It is located in the Landers 9th Avenue Corner 36th Street Fort Bonifacio. The company manages showroom and warehouse stocks while also handling both offline and online sales channels. Sales transactions are processed using an SAP system, while inventory monitoring and procurement tracking are still performed manually through written records and spreadsheets.
Due to the nature of furniture products being high-value items, accurate monitoring of stock levels and allocations is important for daily operations. The company also maintains customer information such as contact details, project locations, and purchase history to support project-based transactions and repeat clients.
II. Problem Statement
Maxxbrands International INC currently faces significant operational challenges due to manual tracking processes that affect both inventory management and procurement operations. These issues result in fragmented data, inefficient tracking, and increased risk of errors across multiple business functions.
Inventory & Sales Management Issues:
High dependency on human input for stock tracking
Risk of data inconsistencies between manual records
Delayed visibility of stock levels
Difficulty tracking showroom vs warehouse stock separately
Manual generation of sales reports
No automated performance analytics
No system alerts when stock reaches minimum threshold
Risk of stockouts and overstocking
Procurement & Supplier Management Issues:
Tracking supplier performance and reliability is difficult without consolidated historical data
Monitoring purchase order status and deliveries requires manual updates across multiple spreadsheets
Managing multiple product pricing across different suppliers lacks centralization
Coordinating payment schedules and terms is time-consuming and error-prone
Generating comprehensive procurement reports requires manual data compilation
The lack of a centralized, integrated database system creates inefficiencies across the entire supply chain, from procurement to sales, hindering the company's ability to make informed, data-driven decisions.
III. Project Description
The Integrated Inventory, Sales & Procurement Management System is a comprehensive database solution designed to address the operational challenges faced by Maxxbrands International INC. This system will replace the current manual, spreadsheet-based approach with an integrated, centralized platform that streamlines inventory management, sales monitoring, supplier relationships, and procurement tracking.
The system consists of two interconnected modules:
Module 1: Inventory & Monitoring System
Centralized product database
Automated sales reports and performance analytics
Real-time stock monitoring across showroom and warehouse locations
Automated stock level alerts and reorder notifications
Module 2: Supplier & Purchase Order Management
Supplier information and contact management
Purchase order creation, approval, and tracking
Product catalogs and supplier-specific pricing
Delivery tracking and receiving processes
Payment schedule and transaction history
System Integration:
Both modules will work together seamlessly within a single database environment. The inventory system will automatically trigger purchase orders when stock levels reach minimum thresholds. Supplier pricing information will feed directly into the product catalog, ensuring consistency across the system. When deliveries are received and logged in the procurement module, warehouse stock levels will be updated automatically in the inventory module. This integrated approach eliminates data duplication and ensures that all departments work from the same real-time information.
Technology Stack:
Oracle Database for storage and management
SQL for database queries, data manipulation, and reporting
Oracle SQL Developer and DBeaver for development and testing
Graphical User Interface using Figma or Canva for design mockups
IV. Project Objectives
General Objective:
To develop an integrated database system that automates inventory management, sales monitoring, supplier relationships, and procurement processes for Maxxbrands International INC, improving operational efficiency and data accuracy.
Specific Objectives:
Design and implement a normalized relational database structure covering products, inventory, sales, suppliers, purchase orders, and payments.
Automate sales reports, performance analytics, and procurement tracking to eliminate manual data compilation.
Implement real-time stock monitoring with automated reorder alerts when inventory reaches minimum thresholds.
Provide visibility of showroom and warehouse stock levels as well as purchase order status through a centralized dashboard.
Create comprehensive digital audit logs for all procurement and inventory activities to support compliance and accountability.
Reduce manual data entry errors across all operations by implementing validation rules and automated data flows.
Develop a user-friendly graphical interface that allows staff to efficiently interact with the system without extensive training.
Generate data-driven insights for inventory replenishment and procurement decisions based on historical trends and current demand patterns.
